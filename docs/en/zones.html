From the surface to the greatest depths

The zones

The water column is divided into depth zones. A whale may move through several zones during its daily, seasonal or feeding movements.

0-200 m

Epipelagic

The sunlit upper zone. Light enables photosynthesis, and most known marine life is concentrated here.

200-1,000 m

Mesopelagic

The twilight zone, where light rapidly fades. Many animals migrate vertically between day and night.

1,000-4,000 m

Bathypelagic

A dark, cold, high-pressure zone beyond the reach of sunlight.

4,000-6,000 m

Abyssopelagic

The zone of the abyssal plains, characterized by permanent darkness, very cold water and extreme pressure.

Below 6,000 m

Hadalpelagic

The deepest zone, restricted to ocean trenches. Its organisms are adapted to extreme conditions.
